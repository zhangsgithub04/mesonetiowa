Locations, units, and time alignment

HRRR, Stage IV, and MRMS each use the nearest point on their own native grid to 41.5868° N, 93.6250° W. Airport weather and gauge rainfall come from KDSM, about 6.3 km away.

Rainfall is a one-hour accumulation in mm. The target and historical HRRR values use forecast hours 1–2 of their respective runs. Stage IV ends at the stated UTC hour. MRMS is radar-only rainfall. The gauge sums 60 valid minute-ending records. Airport temperature and dew point are in °C, and wind components are in m/s, positive east or north.

All joins use hour-ending UTC. Chicago time is displayed for context. Historical airport weather is a report within each input hour, and the most recent input hour ends one hour before the modeled issue time.
